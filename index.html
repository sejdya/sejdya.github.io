WHO WE ARE

A shared commitment to dignity, opportunity and participation.

Society for Equity, Justice and Youth Development Association (SEJYDA) is a social development forum focused on supporting equitable, inclusive and community-driven development.

Through collaboration and local initiative, the Association seeks to create meaningful opportunities for young people and communities. This website will be updated as the organization’s programs and institutional information are confirmed.

◎

Our Vision

An equitable, just and inclusive society where all people can participate and access opportunities.

↗

Our Mission

To encourage youth leadership, community participation, social justice and sustainable development through inclusive initiatives.

✧

Our Values

Equality, dignity, transparency, accountability, participation, respect and inclusion.
